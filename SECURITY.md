# Security

AnyDownload does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.
All link analysis and downloading happens locally, on your device.

If you find a security vulnerability, open a private advisory at
https://github.com/m55681518-byte/AnyDownload/security or email the repository owner.
Do not post exploit details publicly before they are fixed.

## Reporting

1. Describe the issue (affected version, steps to reproduce).
2. Do not include raw credentials or private linked accounts.
3. Expect a response within 7 days.
