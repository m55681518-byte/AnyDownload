# Changelog

## AnyDownload 1.0.0 (2026-09-13)

First release. Rebrand and slim fork of YTDLnis v1.9.0.

- Paste any video link — analyze, pick quality, download. Fully local on your device.
- yt-dlp + ffmpeg + aria2c engines bundled in the APK.
- Downloads save to `Download/AnyDownload/`.
- 1,000+ sites supported by the yt-dlp extraction engine.
